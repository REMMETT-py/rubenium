FAQ

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no licence key, no ads. Every feature is unlocked.

Why does Windows show a warning the first time?

Rubenium is a small independent app without a paid publisher certificate, so Windows SmartScreen does not know it yet. Press “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

I can't see the crosshair or the stats in my game.

Set your game to “borderless” or “windowed fullscreen”. Exclusive fullscreen draws over every other window.

Which music apps work?

Any app that shows up in the Windows media controls: Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, TIDAL, VLC, and YouTube or SoundCloud in a browser.

How do updates work?

Rubenium checks for a new version by itself and shows “Update available” on the card. Press Update and it restarts on the new version.
