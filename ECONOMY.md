# Economy

Every number lives in the `rb_config` table on the server (change it there, no app update needed).

## Earning
| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| New account | 500 coins + 3 keys |
| Time with the app in use | 10 coins every 15 minutes (40 an hour), at most 400 a day |
| Keys | 1 every hour in use, stops at 48 held |
| Old progress (once) | cosmetics kept where they still exist; coins capped at 2 000 |

"In use" = music playing, or mouse/keyboard used in the last 10 minutes. Only the server's clock counts, one
minute per minute, one PC per account at a time. Leaving the PC on overnight earns nothing once input stops and
music is off, and never more than 400 coins a day.

## Rarities and prices
| Rarity | Shop price | Vendor pays (45%) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 200 | 90 |
| Rare | 600 | 270 |
| Special | 1 500 | 675 |
| Epic | 3 500 | 1 575 |
| Exotic | not sold (cases and trading) | 5 400 and up |

- Badges: grey is always Standard. The 13 colours are the badge's own rarity (badges 1-15 Standard, 16-30 Rare,
  31-42 Special, 43-50 Epic). Black, White and Gold are one rarity higher (Epic at most) and only come from cases.
- The 5 Exotic badges: one Epic form, and Black / White / Gold are Exotic. Cases and trading only.
- Balls, lines, fonts: grey costs 20% less; Black / White are one rarity higher at +25%, Gold at +60%.
- Shop themes: 720, 1 800 or 4 200 coins by rarity.

## What a player can expect
- 1 hour a day: 40 coins + 1 key. A Standard item every 5 days, a Rare every 2 weeks from time alone.
- 2 hours a day: 80 coins + 2 keys. Quests, the minigame, the free spin and the daily case (next stage) are
  meant to be the main income: together about 300-500 coins a day for an active player, so a Rare every day or
  two and an Epic in one to two weeks.
- Coins leave through the shop, case fees, the casino's house edge, the market fee and the vendor's 55% cut.

## Cases (1 key + coins each)
Odds are shown in the app. "Value back" is the average worth of what comes out, measured over thousands of
openings on a test database; selling the drops to the vendor returns under half of that, so cases never print coins.

| Case | Price | Coins | Standard | Rare | Special | Epic | Exotic | Guaranteed | Value back |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 100 | 79% | 18.3% | 2.45% | 0.25% | - | - | Rare or better within 30 | about 90% |
| Standard | 300 | 61% | 27.5% | 9% | 2.2% | 0.3% | - | Special or better within 25 | about 89% |
| Premium | 800 | 35.55% | 20% | 30% | 10.5% | 3.7% | 0.25% | Special or better within 10 | about 90% |
| Vault | 2 000 | 20% | - | 37.5% | 27% | 14% | 1.5% | Epic or better within 10 | about 85% |

Exotic badges only come from Premium and Vault. Black, White and Gold badges only come from cases.

## Free things
- Free spin every 3 hours: 20 to 500 coins or a key (about 60 coins on average). The prize waits for "Collect".
- Daily case every 24 hours: 60% coins (30-120), 15% a key, 20% Standard badge, 4.5% Rare, 0.5% Special.
