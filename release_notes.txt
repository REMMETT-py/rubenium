Rubenium 1.3

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- Coins replace hours: 25 coins every 15 minutes Rubenium is open, with a timer for the next drop. Hours already played became coins, and what was already unlocked stays yours.
- Shop: themes, ball styles, rings, colours, badges and titles are bought with coins. Themes are bought in order.
- Colours: white is free. Buy the 16 little squares one by one; with all 16 they melt into the full colour line. Then the same for the second colour.
- Games: blackjack, chicken, wheel and slots, played with coins only. No debt. One free spin a day.
- Badges and titles show on the music tab.
- Tidier menu: Screen = crosshair + display. PC = stats, tools and apps.
- Resetting the settings takes two clicks and a hold. It never touches coins or what you bought.
- Rocket League: Strongline removed. Lantern Lift, Monsoon and Electric banner are marked Coming soon (switched back to the original automatically).
