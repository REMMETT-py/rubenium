Rubenium 4.3

- XP: nothing for a lost bet and only a little for a win (1 to 5), every source slower, at most 1 000 XP a day. XP coupons are worth 40 to 600
- Coinflip bots remade: always 5 to 10 bot flips open, low, middle and high bots, sized to the wealth of the players online (one player: sized to them; a crowd: the server's average). No more Mythic pots
- New casino game: Slots. Three reels, one clear table of prizes, about 96.6% back over time
- New: Wave Rush. Hold to rise, let go to fall; the course narrows, twists and grows spikes the further you go. Solo pays by distance (the server replays every run), or duel someone on the same course with or without a bet
- Mini Golf: Solo (5 holes, no bet, coins by strokes) or Duel as before
- Quick sell is the pawn shop: take his offer or haggle with the slider. Push him too far and he throws you out for 30 minutes
- Social: one Users tab (friends first, with a blue dot), Leaderboards in the tabs, and a clear "Leave online" button
- Cases: badge cases first, odds evened out, Open shows only when you have the case, no more "(no key)"
- Games lobby tidied (coins and Quests on one line, the three freebies side by side); the online menu is taller
- Compact Music Mode only folds while you are away from Rubenium; hover it and everything fades back in
- Patch notes rewritten: one entry per release since 0.1, nothing about features that are gone
