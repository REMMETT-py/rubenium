Rubenium 1.7

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- Accounts: create an account or log in on the welcome screen. Launch unlocks once you are logged in.
- You stay logged in after closing Rubenium or restarting the PC. Log out is under the welcome card and in Settings.
- Username: 3 to 10 letters or numbers. Password: 8 characters or more. There is no password recovery, so remember it.
- Time played is now counted online for your account.
- One look for everyone: strong glow, black background, round corners.
