Rubenium 6.2

- NEW games: Colour Pick (recreate a colour from memory) and SFX Match (recreate a sound). Easy, Medium and Hard, rewards by accuracy, with their own leaderboards
- A big 30 second countdown on every move in Connect 4 and the Cup Game: when it runs out you lose the round
- Cup Game is much harder: more swaps, and faster
- Cases: Mythics are listed in every case as a hidden "?" until you unlock them, and Prismatic chances show above Gold
- Much rarer odds: Mythic 1 / 100 000 in the Top Tier Case and 1 / 250 000 to 1 / 500 000 in the others. Shiny, Titanium and Gold are far rarer too, Painted is the common finish
- Updates no longer pop Rubenium open: after an automatic update it slides to the top right as the ball and waits. Open it to see what is new
