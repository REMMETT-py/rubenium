Rubenium 1.2

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- Games: Rubenium ignores the mouse while a game uses it to aim or turn the camera, also in borderless fullscreen and in games that keep an invisible pointer. In game menus it works as normal.
- Moving the menu: hold it, and the spot you hold stays exactly under the mouse. It follows smoothly, even with fast moves.
- The ball can never sit in the top-right corner of a screen, over the close buttons of windows.
- The welcome-screen code only lasts until Rubenium is closed.
