Rubenium 1.6

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- Rocket League: Lantern Lift and Monsoon no longer crash the game (new files for the current game version). If you had them on, the new files are put in automatically.
- Timer is now at the bottom of the Screen tab. On the PC tab, Tools come after Apps.
- Ball and ring styles: Classic, Outline, Neon, the new White Gold, and Gold.
- Long song names get smaller to fit instead of being cut. Medal is no longer picked up as a music app.
- Badges on the music tab have a little room from the edge.
- Colours: when a plain colour is picked, the slider rests (knob off the line, no value). When the slider is used, the colour dots are greyed a little.
- "Use the colours of the music" now just shows how far you are (e.g. 0.4 / 1 hour to unlock).
