Rubenium 2.3.0

- Starts more reliably with Windows: every part starts on its own, the music connection and the tray icon retry if Windows is still loading, and screen colours are applied again once Windows has settled.
- If something ever fails at start-up, the ball still appears and the problem is written to the log.
- The Rubenium name in the card is drawn as one word (no more tiny gap).
- Every version is now launched and tested on Windows before it is published.
