Rubenium 4.7

- Cases: every badge has new odds. No badge you can get from a case is rarer than 1 in 250 for Basic, 1 in 1 000 for Painted, 1 in 10 000 for Shiny, 1 in 50 000 for Titanium and 1 in 100 000 for Gold, and lower rarities are a bit easier. Cheap cases hold the lower rarities, big cases go up to Epic Gold. What a case pays on average stays the same; Mythic drops are not changed. Every chance shown in the case and on each badge comes from the new table
- Golden Wheel: it now spins inside the menu like the free wheel (no more flashing over online mode) and pays about 3.7 times more: 10 000, 20 000, 40 000, 100 000 or 250 000 coins, or an Elite, Vault, Top Tier or Royal case with a key
- Pawn shop: the price slider starts on his own price (100% yes). Asking for more only asks: when he agrees, press Sell to sell. The item's market price is shown so you can compare
- Game mode no longer closes Rubenium: the menu stays as it is and only stops reacting to the mouse (no hover, no pointer over it). Hold to open and the show / hide key are only in the game mode window now
- Gifting a case: tap a friend and choose 1 to 3, no more typing a username
- Deals: each deal is one per day at its price (it was unlimited)
- Blackjack, Bombs and Hi-Lo: clearer boards with labelled numbers (multiplier, next gem, cash out, streak)
- Fixed: the coinflip chance bar didn't draw; the Medium / Large online menu switch was invisible
- Limits against spam on trades, the market, coinflips, duels, messages and gifts
- Ruben's cheats are gone
