Rubenium 2.1

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- Quests (Games tab): three goals every day and three every week that pay coins. Progress is counted by the server from what you really played.
- Friends: a refresh button on the list and on profiles (it also refreshes your own coins and time played).
- Profiles now show a friend's badges, with the three they have on show first.
