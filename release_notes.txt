Rubenium 3.0 - the full release

CASES
- Style cases: every ball, line, font, theme, banner and frame can come from a case (Style, Theme and Profile cases)
- Coin cases (Starter, Standard, Premium, Jackpot, Titanium) pay back a little more than they cost on average; you still lose about half the time
- Rare hunts (All or Nothing, Painter, Legend Vault, Mythic Hunt) are for rare badges: rare badges are NOT easier to get
- Rare drops feel rare: the strip shows the real odds and never repeats an item; only Epic or better get rays, flashes and fanfare
- Every coin drop has a 6% chance to be a lucky x3; the Jackpot can pay up to 32 times its price
- Every badge shows its exact chance in each case, for every colour and finish
- Gifting a case costs its price and 1 key

PROFILES
- Banners, frames and a showcase of your 3 favourite badges, seen by everyone
- New: Jungle, Sakura, Ember, Nebula banners; Vine, Star, Halo frames (winter looks are saved for the Christmas event)
- Free OG tag for every user who joined before 17 November

ECONOMY
- Fresh start: everyone begins again with 500 coins and 3 keys
- AFK money: 150 coins an hour (up to 1 800 a day) and 1 key an hour
- Badges in the shop cost more, the rarer and luckier they are; the market can go up to the shop price
- Quick sell: sell any item right away for a quarter of its value, or every extra copy at once
