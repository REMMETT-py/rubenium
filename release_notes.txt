Rubenium 1.5

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- Rocket League: Lantern Lift, Monsoon and the Electric banner are back (no longer Coming soon).
- "Turn off all swaps" is now "Reset Swaps": every swap goes off and every original file comes back.
- Automatic repair: a swap you did not choose (left over, from another tool, or the wrong one), or a broken game file, is found and the right file is put back. This runs for everyone, also new users, whenever the game is closed.
- Rubenium now ships clean original files to fall back on when your own saved copy is missing or damaged.
