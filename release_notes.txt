Rubenium 1.4

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

A fresh start: with this update everyone begins again from zero coins and nothing unlocked. From now on everything you earn is kept.

- Casino lobby: the Games tab opens on a small lobby (coins, keys, daily spin). The menu only grows when you enter the casino, and goes back to its size when you leave.
- New games: Plinko (3 risk levels) and Bombs (find the R gems, pick 1 to 20 bombs). They replace Chicken and the Wheel.
- Slots show every prize, and light up the one you hit.
- Badges: 50 badges, each in 17 colours. Basic colours are bought; Rare, Very rare and Gold only come out of cases.
- Cases: 5 cases with their real odds shown, and an opening animation. Each case costs coins and 1 key. Keys are earned by playing in the casino (1 for every 1 000 coins played, up to 10). A badge you already have turns into coins.
- Show up to 3 badges on the music tab. Titles are gone.
- New Neon ball style and ring (5 of each now, Gold is the best).
- Colours cost 100 coins each. White or black, or a colour from the line: one or the other.
- The music's colours open for free after 1 hour with Rubenium.
