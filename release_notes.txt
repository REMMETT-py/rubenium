Rubenium 5.2

- NEW: JACKPOT, at the top of Games. Every 15 minutes, 2 minutes to put in up to 3 items; your chance is your share of the total. Then bots fill the empty seats, the wheel spins for 10 seconds and the winner gets their items back and picks half of everyone else's
- Leaderboard titles: be top 3 of a leaderboard to wear "#1 RICH" in gold, #2 in silver, #3 in bronze. They follow the leaderboards live. Wear up to 3 titles with your OG tag (Style > Your profile, or Inventory > Profile)
- Coinflip: when the last 15 seconds start, the screen fades into a face-off: you on top, them below, the coin in the middle and a glowing countdown
- Mini Golf: aim with the mouse while the power bar swings by itself; click or press Space at the right moment. Your ball and whose turn it is are much easier to see
