Rubenium 1.1

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- RL Swap: 6 new swaps (Purple Standard boost, Affluenter, Black Infinium, Alpha wheels, S8 SSL banner) and a new Player banners section. Swaps now also work for items the game has not downloaded yet.
- Ball: stays where you leave it, snaps to a corner or the middle of an edge only when dropped close to one, and works on every monitor.
- Games: while a game hides or locks the mouse, clicks go through Rubenium to the game, so it can't be opened or dragged by accident.
- Display: just Default and Custom. Moving a slider switches to Custom; your settings are saved.
- Apps: finds every game installed with Steam or Epic.
- Stats: fully unloaded when you leave the tab.
- Themes: Mythic replaces Prism, new R styles (Bloom, Shine) and rings (Comet, Aura).
