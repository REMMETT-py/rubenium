Rubenium 8.0

- A cleaner look everywhere: every button, tab, card and text field now shares one set of corners, surfaces and spacing
- Reduce motion: a new switch in Settings > Style makes interface animations shorter and calmer
- Empty pages now say what goes there and how to fill them
- Game tabs show their full names, and every row of buttons is even
- Lighter: Rubenium uses much less processor while it rests
- Mini Golf online is fair for everyone: the server plays out every shot with the game's own physics
- Safer accounts: password help is limited per PC and per name
- Solo Mini Golf online needs Rubenium 8.0
