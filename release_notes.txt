Rubenium 1.10

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- Logged in? No button to press: a line runs around the welcome card for 5 seconds, then Rubenium starts. Click the card to start at once.
- Logged out or no account: the card asks you to log in, as before.
