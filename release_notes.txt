Rubenium 1.0

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- Hold to open: hold the ball to open Rubenium, so it never opens by accident in a game. The hold time is in Settings, and it can be switched off.
- Hold the menu for a moment to move it.
- RL Swap: 9 new swaps (Lantern Lift, Monsoon, Cosmological, Strongline, Ombre, Sparkles, Anodized Pearl, Electric and S21 SSL banners), separate Fennec and Octane decals, and a Paint finishes section.
- Apps: open apps move to the top.
- Themes: new white styles for the R (Echo, Stripes).
- Updates tab: the full history of every version.
