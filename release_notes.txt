Rubenium 1.1

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- Themes rebuilt: one night sky that fills with stars as the hours go by, then turns to gold (2, 6, 12, 24, 48, 72, 100, 150, 200 and 250 h).
- Colours: pick any colour on a colour line, and add a second colour after 24 h. "Use the colours of the music" is now at the top of Themes.
- Ball: R styles Classic, Outline, Echo, Gold; rings Dots, Line, Comet, Gold.
- Rubenium start screen when Rocket League and Fortnite load (can be switched off in Settings > Games).
- Softer interface sounds, a smooth menu size change, and a new app icon.
- RL Swap: the Electric banner now replaces the Diamond Tile banner.
