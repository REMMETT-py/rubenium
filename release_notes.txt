Rubenium 2.0 - the online update

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

Everyone starts fresh: 500 coins and 3 keys. Coins, keys and items now live on your account, not on the PC.

- Coins come with time: 10 every 15 minutes with Rubenium in use (up to 400 a day), and 1 key every hour.
- Five rarities: Standard, Rare, Special, Epic and Exotic. 50 badges ranked from Heart to Sunburst Star, each in grey, 13 colours and Black / White / Gold, plus 5 Exotic badges that only come from cases.
- Badges: buy colours with coins, show 3 on the music tab, sell any badge to the vendor for quick coins. A badge you no longer have stays in the collection, greyed out.
- Four new cases (1 key + coins) with their real odds shown, and a free daily case.
- Casino played on the server: Blackjack, Plinko, Bombs, Slots, and two new games, Dice and Rocket. Bets from 10 to 1 000.
- Free spin every 3 hours: the prize waits until you press Collect.
- Themes (8), the ball (3) and its ring (3) now unlock with hours played. Colours are free.
- The shop and the casino need an internet connection. Music, crosshair and swaps work offline as before.
