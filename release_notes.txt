Rubenium 1.8

Already have Rubenium? Open it, go to Settings and press Check for updates.
New here? Download RubeniumSetup.exe below (no admin rights needed, removable from Settings > Apps).

- The login now sits inside the start screen: the card opens to show it and closes again once you are logged in.
- Delete your account in Settings: type your username, then click twice and hold.
- "Reset all settings" is gone (each tab keeps its own reset). Quit moved to the very bottom of Settings.
- Rocket League: the Lantern Lift, Monsoon and Electric banner swaps were removed. If one was on, the original file is put back automatically.
